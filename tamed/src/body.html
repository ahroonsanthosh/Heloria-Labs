01Voice practice for sales teams

Kríos

Reps rehearse the calls that matter against a buyer who answers back, before a real one hears them.

The problem

The calls that matter are the hardest ones to practise. Too often, the first time a rep hears the hard question, a real buyer is asking it.

What it does

Kríos is voice practice for sales teams. A rep talks the call through out loud, and the buyer on the other end answers back. The call gets rehearsed before a real buyer ever hears it.

Who leads it

Abraham Sabu Kurian

Where it lives

02Counter-drone command software

Aetos

When the sky fills with drones, the hard part is not shooting. It is knowing which one to shoot.

The problem

A crowded sky is a hard thing to read. Friend and threat look alike on a screen, and the decision has to be right.

What it does

Aetos is counter-drone command software. It reads the radar, identifies what is in the sky and intercepts the threat, and it never hits a friendly.

Who leads it

Ahroon Santhosh

Where it lives

github.com/ahroonsanthosh/Aetos

03GPS-free drone navigation

Pharos

When GPS is jammed, spoofed or gone, it keeps going. And it knows when it doesn't know.

The problem

A drone that leans on GPS is lost the moment the signal is jammed, spoofed or gone.

What it does

Pharos is GPS-free navigation for drones. It finds where the drone is by matching its camera view to the map, and it is honest about the moments it cannot be sure.

Who leads it

Ahroon Santhosh

Where it lives

github.com/ahroonsanthosh/pharos

04AI tools for OpenRocket

Ogive

Say what you want the rocket to do, and let it work the design until it does.

The problem

Getting a rocket design to do what you want usually means changing it by hand, again and again.

What it does

Ogive is a set of AI tools for OpenRocket. You say what you want the rocket to do, and it works the design until it does.

Who leads it

Michael Sweeney Heffernan

Where it lives

github.com/michaelsheffernan/ogive
